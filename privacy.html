Privacy Notice

FiberBro Internet Communication Services Corp. · Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

Please have this notice reviewed by your counsel or a Data Protection Officer before relying on it as a final legal document. This page is a short, plain-language draft for the website inquiry form. No NPC registration numbers are claimed here.

1. Who we are

FiberBro Internet Communication Services Corp. (“FiberBro”, “we”, “us”) provides fiber internet services exclusively in Payatas, Quezon City, Philippines.

Contact for privacy and inquiries: fiberbropayatas@gmail.com

2. What personal data we collect

When you use our website inquiry form, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Address or landmark in Payatas
  • Plan of interest and your message
  • Your consent to this notice

We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

3. Why we collect it (purpose)

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Check whether fiber service is available at your location in Payatas
  • Follow up about plan options and installation (including the one-time ₱1,000 installation fee)

We process this data based on your consent (the checkbox on the form) and our legitimate interest in answering customer requests.

4. Who processes the data

The inquiry form is delivered through Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script, under the company's Google account), which receives the form submission and stores it for us. Google acts as a personal information processor for this purpose. Please also review Google’s own privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Inquiry records are kept for up to 12 months unless you become a subscriber, in which case relevant details may be kept longer as needed to provide service and meet legal or accounting requirements. You may ask us to delete your inquiry sooner (see rights below).

6. Your rights (data subjects)

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated data
  • Erase or block data that is no longer needed, subject to legal limits
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for processing based on consent
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

To exercise these rights, email fiberbropayatas@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request”.

National Privacy Commission: privacy.gov.ph

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect inquiry data in transit (this site uses HTTPS when hosted on Cloudflare Pages) and limit access to people who need it to respond to you. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

8. Updates

We may update this notice from time to time. The version on this page is the current one. Last updated: November 2026.
